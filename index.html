Hi, I’m Kelly—
an illustrator between reality and imagination.

Hi, I'm Kelly Yin, an illustrator who enjoys exploring the space between reality and imagination.

My work combines surrealism, anatomy, and unexpected visual elements to explore human emotions, identity, and social issues. I'm drawn to the playful yet unsettling—creating images that feel whimsical at first glance but reveal something strange or thought-provoking beneath the surface.

Through my illustrations, I hope to spark curiosity, challenge familiar perspectives, and invite viewers to find beauty in the unusual.
